# Historical continuity

CircuitNET NG is a modern successor inspired by CircuitNET and CircuitNet
International, the BBS network associated with historical SPITFIRE systems. It
carries forward independent Sysops, ROOT/HOST/END relationships, codenames,
Dossiers and published conference changes. It is not a reprint of the old network
Charter or a claim of endorsement or legal succession.

A retained official conference list and its change notices reconcile to 71
historical records: 60 active, five deleted and six rejected. The list already
included all 23 additions described in the retained change notice. These counts
remain historical; the modern catalog has 46 adopted areas with their own scopes.
No original proprietary manuals, programs or research samples are in this kit.

The first four historical areas were classified for Sysops and visiting Sysops:
SUPPORT, SYSOP, SPITFIRE and DOORS. The modern catalog preserves that restriction.
SUPPORT is core; the other three are optional. Local boards implement access and
verify visiting operators. Catalog availability never grants caller privileges.

The modern Charter allows handles without automatically disclosing private real
names. This is an explicit departure from the historical real-identity-in-body
rule. Modern encrypted links protect transport, not secrecy after delivery.

The rights-safe adoption review is supplied as config/catalog-review.json.
Public project documentation and further historical summaries are available from
https://github.com/cdaters/spitfire-ng in its docs/ directory. Historical records
and modern decisions remain separately labeled in that documentation.
