# Network goals

CircuitNET NG brings BBS operators and callers together for conversation,
experimentation, preservation and shared interests.

- Keep BBS communication useful and enjoyable.
- Preserve recognizable BBS practices while supporting current systems.
- Make room for learning, practical projects and independent implementations.
- Respect the independence of participating Sysops and their local boards.
- Publish clear conference scopes, operating rules and network changes.
- Handle disagreement and mistakes fairly, without harassment or disruption.

The [Charter](CHARTER.md) describes how the network makes decisions.
The [Rules](RULES.md) describe everyday participation.
