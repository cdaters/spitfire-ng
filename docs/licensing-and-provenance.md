resources are available under **MIT OR Apache-2.0**, at your option. The full
terms are in [LICENSE-MIT](../LICENSE-MIT) and
[LICENSE-APACHE](../LICENSE-APACHE).

That project license covers only material owned by the SPITFIRE NG project
unless a component says otherwise. It does not relicense:

- original Buffalo Creek Software programs, manuals, DISPLAY resources, or
  registered/private binaries;
- historical archives used as read-only research evidence;
- Synchronet or other third-party source and research material; or
- independently distributed community packages with their own terms.

Those materials retain their original copyrights and licenses. Proprietary
historical inputs are not part of this public source tree or the Development
Preview package.

## Presentation and language packages

Every presentation profile and language package carries package-level
licensing, provenance, inventory, and hash metadata. This lets future package
authors choose compatible terms without changing SPITFIRE NG's engine
license. Installing a package never gives its strings or artwork command,
authorization, database, or filesystem authority.

The bundled Modern, Classic SPITFIRE-inspired, and Minimal Terminal profiles
and the en-US language package contain independently authored project
resources. The Classic profile is historically informed but does not contain
original Buffalo Creek DISPLAY, HLP, MNU, or RIP bytes.

## Historical research

Compatibility work may use legally held local historical material as
read-only evidence. Public documentation records independently written
findings, hashes, and provenance where useful; it does not turn the source
repository into a historical software archive.

For original software, manuals, and preservation downloads, visit
[Original SPITFIRE Software & Documentation](https://spitfirebbs.com/).

Contributors must not submit proprietary binaries, registered copies, private
caller data, or artwork they cannot redistribute. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for the evidence and contribution rules.
